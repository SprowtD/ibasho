## Default routing this suggests

1. OpenRouter as the one-key default for text, pictures and the video models it carries.
2. Google key for voices (cheapest), Nano Banana, Omni video and Lyria music.
3. MiniMax direct for H3 when audio or references matter; fal for H3 at 1080p and multi-reference Seedance.
4. ElevenLabs for cloned voices, sound effects and music; MiniMax as the second voice option.
5. Account sign-ins (Higgsfield, Runway) for people who already pay for them; ComfyUI and RunPod for open models.
6. Never Sora; never depend on Veo; never Kling direct.
